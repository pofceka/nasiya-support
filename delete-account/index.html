Delete your Nasiya account

You can request account deletion by email. You do not need to install Nasiya or sign in to this website.

Send a deletion request

  1. Email nasiyailova@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Nasiya account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Nasiya account and say that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
  3. We will verify account ownership and contact you about completion. Never send your password or verification codes.
Email deletion request

If you cannot access your account email, explain this in your request so we can discuss verification.

What deletion covers

Account deletion removes your login and store membership, invitations associated with your account, and voice usage records. Shared ledger entries needed by other store members remain, with your account identity and authorship removed. If you are the last member of a store, its ledger is deleted along with the store.

Before you request deletion

Deletion is permanent. Keep any records you need and synchronize pending entries first. Customer names, phone numbers, and free-text notes in a shared ledger are business records used by the remaining members. Remove personal information you do not want retained in those records, or describe it in your request.

Device copies and backups

Offline devices and files previously exported or shared can retain copies. Sign out on your other devices to clear their local app caches. See the retention policy for backup and service-log handling.

The email button opens your email app. Your request is sent only when you send that email; opening this page does not delete your account.
