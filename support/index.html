How can we help?

Help with your shop ledger, account, and everyday use of Nasiya.

Contact Nasiya support

Email Ayubxon Yuldashev at nasiyailova@gmail.com.

Email support

Include your app version, phone model, and a short description of the problem. Never send your password or customers’ private information.

Sign-in and account recovery

Use the email address and password for your Nasiya account. You can open the sign-in page for account recovery. If you cannot sign in or a recovery email does not arrive, contact support.

Offline records and synchronization

Reconnect to the internet and allow your changes to synchronize before signing out or reinstalling. Signing out clears this device’s cached ledger and pending changes. Keep a separate copy of any entries that have not synchronized.

Voice entry

Allow microphone access in your phone’s settings. Recognition availability depends on the device and selected language. Review and edit the transcript before saving. You can always enter records manually.

Privacy and account deletion

Read how Nasiya handles your information in our privacy policy. To leave Nasiya, request account deletion by email without reinstalling the app.
