Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Nasiya is a shared shop ledger for Android and iOS. Ayubxon Yuldashev is responsible for Nasiya. For privacy questions, contact nasiyailova@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

Nasiya processes your email address, account identifier, authentication information, store membership, and ledger records. Ledger records can include customer names, phone numbers, notes, debts, repayments, dates, currencies, and entry authorship. Only record customer information you are entitled to use.

We use this information to sign you in, synchronize records, calculate balances, show history, and manage store invitations. Other members of your store can see its shared ledger and member email addresses.

Services and sharing

Supabase provides account services and synchronized data storage. Vercel hosts the app’s API, sign-in, and invitation pages. GitHub Pages hosts these public information pages. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, and service logs. Support messages are processed through Gmail. We do not sell your personal information.

These pages do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. The current Nasiya app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or payment collection.

Optional voice input

Voice entry requires microphone permission. Uzbek recognition on iOS uses a downloaded on-device model. Russian recognition on iOS and recognition on Android use the system speech service, which may send audio to Apple, Google, or the configured provider depending on device settings and availability. Review the provider’s privacy settings for its handling of audio.

You can edit the transcript and review each proposed entry before saving it. Saved text becomes part of the ledger. Current mobile voice entry does not send the transcript to an AI field-extraction service. Manual entry remains available.

Information on your device

The app caches your signed-in ledger and pending changes for offline use. Authentication tokens use the device’s secure storage. Signing out clears the app’s account cache on that device. Your operating system’s backup settings may retain device data. Exports and information shared with other apps are controlled by you and the receiving service.

Retention and deletion

Account information is retained while your account is active. Ledger records remain while the store uses them, unless deleted. To request deletion of your account or associated personal information, follow the account deletion instructions.

Deleting your account removes your login, membership, associated invitations, and voice usage records. Shared ledgers used by other members remain with your account identity and authorship removed. If you are the last store member, the store and its ledger are deleted. Customer details and free-text notes in shared records remain available to other members unless removed separately.

Offline device caches and exported copies cannot be remotely recalled. Sign out on other devices to clear their local app caches.

Backup copies and technical service logs, where retained by our providers, follow the providers’ retention schedules rather than immediate deletion from the active database. Nasiya’s Supabase project currently uses the Free plan, which does not include automatic database backups or point-in-time recovery. Supabase publishes a one-day retention period for API and database logs on that plan, and one hour for Auth audit logs. See backup information and Supabase retention limits. These service-log limits do not describe data stored in the application database or copies held by other providers. We do not promise that all provider copies disappear immediately after an account is deleted.

Support correspondence is used to handle your request and any follow-up. You may ask us to delete that correspondence by emailing the support address.

Your choices and questions

You can decline microphone access and enter records manually. Contact nasiyailova@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, or to ask about this policy. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on a request.

Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
